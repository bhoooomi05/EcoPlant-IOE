EcoPlant

Version 1.0.0 · Smart Plant IoT System

EcoPlant pairs an ESP8266 microcontroller with soil, temperature, humidity and water-level sensors to keep your plants thriving. It automates irrigation, tracks trends over time and sends you alerts before small problems become big ones.

Key Features

Automated IrrigationWaters your plants based on live sensor thresholds.
Real-Time MonitoringTrack soil moisture, temperature, humidity and tank level.
Smart AlertsGet notified the moment something needs attention.
Reports & HistoryReview long-term trends and export summaries.
